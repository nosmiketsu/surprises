Hi Babe,

I know we've only been dating for two months now, and I know it's kind of cheesy for me to say that I already know how you feel. Ang gusto ko lang sabihin is mahal kita despite these early months.

I know we have our what-ifs — especially if tayo yung nagsimula earlier. And I keep telling you na iyong mga what-ifs, this time, hindi na. Kasi ito na. Andito na tayong dalawa.

But I also know that what you have experienced and what you're carrying right now, I can't simply replace. I can't erase the things that happened before me, and I don't expect myself to.

Ang gusto ko lang malaman mo is hindi mo kailangang dalhin lahat mag-isa.

That's why I keep telling you — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or even on random days — if you need me, I am always here. And this is the behind of every "Kamusta ka po babe?, Kamusta ka?"

Sana kapag dumating sa point na you feel down, depressed, stressed, anxious, or even happy and excited, or kahit ano pa man 'yan, alam mong nandito ako. Willing akong makinig sa kwento mo, sa rant mo sa buhay, sa problema mo, or kahit kanino man 'yan.

This time, you have a partner who is ready to listen to everything and ready to ask, "Kailangan mo ba ng tulong?" Hindi dahil kailangan kong ayusin lahat para sa'yo, but because I want you to know that you don't have to face every difficult day alone.

Mahal kita, Babe. I know hindi ko ito palaging nasasabi vocally, but this is my way of saying it to you.

And please remember this, Babe:

You are an amazing woman. And I genuinely believe you can reach everything you want in life.

Alam ko na kaya mong marating lahat ng gusto mo without doubts. Kaya mong gawin lahat ng plano mo. Kaya mong makuha at mabili ang mga bagay na pinapangarap mo. Your plans may have taken a different path, and some things may have been delayed, but that doesn't mean they are gone.

Graduation, work, career, review center, board license — lahat ng 'yan, darating din. Hindi pa tapos ang story mo.

And ako, andito lang para sumuporta sa'yo. Sasamahan kita sa lahat ng gusto mong gawin. I will celebrate your wins, listen to your bad days, and remind you of how capable you are whenever you start doubting yourself.

Kasi iyon ang role ko para sa'yo — gabayan ka, suportahan ka, at protektahan ka. Not to control your life, but to stand beside you while you build the life you want.

Mahal kita, Babe.

I always love you, no matter what.

— Always here for you ♥
